Privacy policy.

What happens to the details you send us, in plain English. Last updated November 12, 2026.

What we collect

When you ask for a quote or send us a message, we receive what you type into the form: your name, phone number, email address, street address or city, property type, the services you are interested in, any promo code, and your message. We do not ask for payment details on this website.

How we use it

We use your details only to answer your request: to call or text you back, prepare a quote, schedule the work and keep a record of the job. We do not sell, rent or trade your information, and we do not add you to a marketing list.

Who handles it

Form submissions are delivered to our inbox by EmailJS, an email delivery service. The contact page shows a Google Map, which is loaded from Google and is subject to Google's privacy policy. This website does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep your messages and job records for as long as we need them to serve you and to meet our business and tax record-keeping obligations.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the information we hold about you at any time. Call or text (859) 248-0576 or email hello@lawnbusterzky.com.
