// About Lawn Busterz

Family-owned.
Boots-on in Kentucky.

Lawn Busterz started in a Richmond, KY garage with a snow plow, a sharp blade, and a phone that wouldn't stop ringing in a storm. We built the rest of the company around the same idea: when something on your property needs to get done, the right crew shows up and does it.

// The Story

It started with a plow.

The first version of Lawn Busterz was a single truck and a snow plow taking 3am calls across Madison County. Driveways, walkways, parking lots — whatever needed clearing before the morning rush.

One winter turned into a full year of property work. Customers asked if we mowed in the summer. Then if we mulched. Then if we could fix the standing water in the back yard. The answer kept being yes, and the equipment list kept growing.

Today Lawn Busterz runs ten core services across Central Kentucky — mowing, mulching, weed control, flea & tick spraying, debris cleanup, snow removal, skid steer, excavator, French drains, and weedeating. Same family. Same number. Same crew that started in the snow.
